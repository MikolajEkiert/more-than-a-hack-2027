- 2026-11-13 · task 01 · The ATD ball speed column (`Match.ball[:, 2]`) is not used by any new method. - 2026-11-13 · task 01 · Ball gaps are all-or-nothing on this tracking: 17 gaps under 1 s, none between 1 and 3 s, 133 of 3 s or more covering 40.7% of graded frames. So there are no short occlusions to bridge and no use for `X`; the question is which long gap is a stoppage and which is play with the ball lost. Gap length does not separate them; the players' speed inside the gap does better (median of the 90th percentile: bulk 1.0-2.0 m/s, tail to 6 m/s, against a play mode near 3-3.5 m/s). This replaces the gap-length mixture proposed in docs/research/UNSUPERVISED_RESEARCH.md section 4.1.

- 2026-11-13 · tasks 03-05 · The decoder is a switching-cost Viterbi, not yet the explicit-duration HSMM of the research document: it is the simplest decoder that removes the lag of the majority vote, and with perfect stage inputs it scores 0.994 on the external match, so the decoder is not what limits the result. Its recurrence is a per-frame Python loop (an exception to the vectorisation rule: three states, sequential by nature; 3.5 s on the match).
- 2026-11-13 · tasks 03-05 · Where the error is, measured on the external match with one stage replaced by the truth: a perfect team signal lifts the control version from 0.50 to 0.88, a perfect dead-ball signal only to 0.57. - 2026-11-13 · task 02 · The external labels exist in two readings of "possession", and both are always scored. `touch` (any on-ball event flips the owner) gives about 6.0 possessions per live minute, mean 10.0-10.5 s; a quarter of its possessions are under 2 s, mostly headers exchanged between the teams. `controlled` (a touch counts only if the team then holds the ball 0.2 s or completes a pass, shot or set piece; CHALLENGE.md's wording) gives 4.03-4.09 per live minute and 14.7-14.9 s on all three matches, against 4.2 and 14.2 s published for the public window. - 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Second version (`configs/unsup_v2.toml`), accepted by the rule fixed in advance under both degradation profiles: mean spellF1 0.598 against 0.545 for the control version under `label_free` (touch +0.045, controlled +0.061, 3 of 3 matches) and 0.448 against 0.397 under `published_baselines` (+0.045, +0.056, 3 of 3). - 2026-11-14 · task 05 · The decoder now switches state on a tie, so a change lands on the first frame with evidence for the new state. Before, ties were broken by state order: home-to-away changes landed at the end of a stretch without evidence and away-to-home ones at its start. This is the provider's rule (possession stays until the opponent touches the ball) and treats both teams alike.
- 2026-11-14 · task 05 · Edges of decoded stoppages are snapped onto the edges of the ball gap (within 3 s): players keep running after the whistle, so evidence from their speed turned a median 17 frames late at the start of a stoppage on the external matches; restarts within 0.5 s of the truth went from 57% to 90%. A restart is moved to the ball's reappearance only if the ball is moving then (a placed ball is not a restart).
- 2026-11-14 · task 03 · Kick-off rule from formation only (`features.kickoffs`): each team in its own half is a kick-off set-up, and play is dead until the kick (the ball leaving the centre mark, or 1.8 s before the set-up ends when the ball is not tracked; that lag is the median on the external matches' kick-off events). On the external matches every kick-off event is found, 0.3 s late on average. On our match it puts the second-half kick-off 0.2 s from the anchor published in `games.py`, and the stretch before it is now all D (it was 63% D with 3 false possessions); no constant of this match is used.
- 2026-11-14 · task 04 · `team.touch_episodes` replaces `control_evidence` in the second version: evidence comes from real touches (the ball moves with the player, or its velocity changes across the contact) and is carried across a completed pass or dribble (two consecutive touches of the same team with the ball tracked in between). Evidence then covers 68% of live frames instead of 45% on the external matches, and is right on 92% instead of 89%.
- 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Tried on the external matches and dropped: giving the team of the last touch evidence until the next touch of either team (creates false short possessions: 0.649 to 0.558-0.644 depending on strength); re-estimating the stoppage cues from the match itself by a plain hard EM (0.649 to 0.625 on the external match, and on our match it returns almost the hand-set values); touch detection from jumps in the ball's velocity (the automatic ball track is too smooth). - 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Where the rest is, on game 3 with one stage replaced by the truth (controlled reading, prototype at 0.65): a perfect team signal on frames with a tracked ball gives 0.84, perfect stoppages 0.73, a perfect team signal on frames without a ball 0.73, everything perfect 0.99. - 2026-11-14 · task 04 · Restart evidence in `team.touch_episodes`: when the ball is tracked again after 2 s or more without it and one team's player stands at it with no opponent within 3 m more, that team is taking the restart and gets evidence until the next real touch. Reason, measured on the external matches: the team in the first 2 s after a true restart was wrong in 26% of restarts (65% after corners), because the ball reappears already in flight and leaves no touch to see, while the taker is by far the nearest player (median 2 m against 11 m for the nearest opponent). With the rule: 11% wrong (corners 15 of 25 wrong before, 4 after). Worth +0.02 (`label_free`) and +0.014 (`published_baselines`); insensitive to its parameters.
- 2026-11-14 · task 03 · A tracked ball moving at 4 m/s or more inside the pitch counts as evidence of play (+2 nats).
