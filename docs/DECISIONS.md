- 2026-11-13 · task 01 · The ATD ball speed column (`Match.ball[:, 2]`) is not used by any new method. It is filled on 7,377 of 94,214 tracked graded frames and its values disagree with the positions (median 1.3 m/s against 5.1 m/s from positions). Ball speed, acceleration and heading change come from `features.ball_kinematics` (quadratic Savitzky-Golay over 7 frames). The player speed column agrees with positions (correlation 0.92) and is used as is.
- 2026-11-13 · task 01 · Ball gaps are all-or-nothing on this tracking: 17 gaps under 1 s, none between 1 and 3 s, 133 of 3 s or more covering 40.7% of graded frames. So there are no short occlusions to bridge and no use for `X`; the question is which long gap is a stoppage and which is play with the ball lost. Gap length does not separate them; the players' speed inside the gap does better (median of the 90th percentile: bulk 1.0-2.0 m/s, tail to 6 m/s, against a play mode near 3-3.5 m/s). This replaces the gap-length mixture proposed in docs/research/UNSUPERVISED_RESEARCH.md section 4.1.

- 2026-11-13 · tasks 03-05 · Control version of the unsupervised pipeline (`configs/unsup_control.toml`): `ball_state.stoppage_evidence` (ball tracked or not, players' speed, ball outside the pitch, ball not moving) + `team.control_evidence` (the nearer team when a player is within 2 m, weak when the ball is passing by, none in a duel) + `decode.switch_cost` (most likely H/A/D path under switching costs, per half, no `X`). Hand-set football priors; the only values chosen on data are the control radius and the three switching costs, on the degraded external match. External validation: spellF1 0.503 [0.466, 0.546] against 0.321 [0.298, 0.350] for `baseline_smooth50`; seeds 1 and 2 give 0.483 and 0.488 against 0.290 and 0.317. On the match: 358 possessions, mean 9.8 s, 44.8% dead (label-free diagnostics). **These external scores are in-sample for the control radius and the three switching costs** (chosen on the same match and labels; seeds 1 and 2 re-degrade that match, they are not held out), while `baseline_smooth50` was not tuned there, so the gap over it is optimistic by an unknown amount. A held-out number (choose on one half, score on the other) is still to be measured, and a ranking claim should rest on it. Runs: `experiments/runs/20261113T2155*` (logged from an uncommitted tree on top of 8fef60b, like task 02's; not reproducible from a commit until re-logged).
- 2026-11-13 · tasks 03-05 · The decoder is a switching-cost Viterbi, not yet the explicit-duration HSMM of the research document: it is the simplest decoder that removes the lag of the majority vote, and with perfect stage inputs it scores 0.994 on the external match, so the decoder is not what limits the result. Its recurrence is a per-frame Python loop (an exception to the vectorisation rule: three states, sequential by nature; 3.5 s on the match).
- 2026-11-13 · tasks 03-05 · Where the error is, measured on the external match with one stage replaced by the truth: a perfect team signal lifts the control version from 0.50 to 0.88, a perfect dead-ball signal only to 0.57. Possessions under 5 s (half of all) are almost all missed. The team stage is the next thing to work on.
- 2026-11-13 · tasks 03-05 · Tried and dropped: touch detection from jumps in the ball's velocity. The automatic ball track is much smoother than the corrected one (velocity changes above 5 m/s: 7.6 per minute against 59 on the external match), so the thresholds do not transfer. This is the default of `validation.degrade` (`ball_smooth_s`), so it moves every external score: the task 02 figures above are superseded (`baseline_smooth50` is now 0.321 [0.298, 0.350] on seed 0, against 0.318).

- 2026-11-13 · task 02 · The external labels exist in two readings of "possession", and both are always scored. `touch` (any on-ball event flips the owner) gives about 6.0 possessions per live minute, mean 10.0-10.5 s; a quarter of its possessions are under 2 s, mostly headers exchanged between the teams. `controlled` (a touch counts only if the team then holds the ball 0.2 s or completes a pass, shot or set piece; CHALLENGE.md's wording) gives 4.03-4.09 per live minute and 14.7-14.9 s on all three matches, against 4.2 and 14.2 s published for the public window. The published full-match baseline scores point the other way (they are reproduced a little better by `touch`), so neither is assumed: a change must not lose under either.
- 2026-11-13 · task 02 · Stoppages in the external labels need confirmation by the events (a restart at the end of a stretch without a ball, a stoppage event at its start, or 3 s without a ball). In games 1-2 the corrected tracking also drops the ball for a moment during play (0.2-0.5% of frames); those stretches stay live.
- 2026-11-13 · task 02 · Two degradation profiles are kept side by side in `configs/degradation/`, by the owner's decision, so the choice is one switch (`--profile`). `label_free`: set from aggregates of our tracking, no labels and no scores; the organiser baseline scores 0.334 on it, against 0.2433 on the real match, so it is too easy. `published_baselines`: fitted by `possession calibrate` so that the four organiser baselines score what EVALUATION.md publishes (RMSE to the eight numbers 0.025, against 0.088 for `label_free`); the baseline scores 0.238 on it. It uses published documentation only, never the ground-truth files; whether that is acceptable is still the team's call. Control pipeline: 0.522 under `label_free`, 0.383 under `published_baselines`; it beats the baseline by 0.19 and 0.15 and passes the acceptance rule under both.
- 2026-11-13 · task 02 · Acceptance rule for every later change, fixed before measuring: mean spellF1 gain of at least 0.02 over the three matches, no loss under either label reading, and a win on at least two of the three matches (`validation.compare`).
- 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Second version (`configs/unsup_v2.toml`), accepted by the rule fixed in advance under both degradation profiles: mean spellF1 0.598 against 0.545 for the control version under `label_free` (touch +0.045, controlled +0.061, 3 of 3 matches) and 0.448 against 0.397 under `published_baselines` (+0.045, +0.056, 3 of 3). The organiser baseline scores 0.334 and 0.238 on the same conditions. Runs: `experiments/runs/20261113T2312*` to `T2315*`.
- 2026-11-14 · task 05 · The decoder now switches state on a tie, so a change lands on the first frame with evidence for the new state. Before, ties were broken by state order: home-to-away changes landed at the end of a stretch without evidence and away-to-home ones at its start. This is the provider's rule (possession stays until the opponent touches the ball) and treats both teams alike.
- 2026-11-14 · task 05 · Edges of decoded stoppages are snapped onto the edges of the ball gap (within 3 s): players keep running after the whistle, so evidence from their speed turned a median 17 frames late at the start of a stoppage on the external matches; restarts within 0.5 s of the truth went from 57% to 90%. A restart is moved to the ball's reappearance only if the ball is moving then (a placed ball is not a restart).
- 2026-11-14 · task 03 · Kick-off rule from formation only (`features.kickoffs`): each team in its own half is a kick-off set-up, and play is dead until the kick (the ball leaving the centre mark, or 1.8 s before the set-up ends when the ball is not tracked; that lag is the median on the external matches' kick-off events). On the external matches every kick-off event is found, 0.3 s late on average. On our match it puts the second-half kick-off 0.2 s from the anchor published in `games.py`, and the stretch before it is now all D (it was 63% D with 3 false possessions); no constant of this match is used.
- 2026-11-14 · task 04 · `team.touch_episodes` replaces `control_evidence` in the second version: evidence comes from real touches (the ball moves with the player, or its velocity changes across the contact) and is carried across a completed pass or dribble (two consecutive touches of the same team with the ball tracked in between). Evidence then covers 68% of live frames instead of 45% on the external matches, and is right on 92% instead of 89%.
- 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Tried on the external matches and dropped: giving the team of the last touch evidence until the next touch of either team (creates false short possessions: 0.649 to 0.558-0.644 depending on strength); re-estimating the stoppage cues from the match itself by a plain hard EM (0.649 to 0.625 on the external match, and on our match it returns almost the hand-set values); touch detection from jumps in the ball's velocity (the automatic ball track is too smooth). Touch-episode parameters (radius 1.5-2.0 m, velocity change 3-6 m/s, bridge 4-8 s) move the score by 0.005-0.01, inside the noise.
- 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Where the rest is, on game 3 with one stage replaced by the truth (controlled reading, prototype at 0.65): a perfect team signal on frames with a tracked ball gives 0.84, perfect stoppages 0.73, a perfect team signal on frames without a ball 0.73, everything perfect 0.99. The team in control of a visible ball is still the main loss; that is where a supervised model could add most.
- 2026-11-14 · task 04 · Restart evidence in `team.touch_episodes`: when the ball is tracked again after 2 s or more without it and one team's player stands at it with no opponent within 3 m more, that team is taking the restart and gets evidence until the next real touch. Reason, measured on the external matches: the team in the first 2 s after a true restart was wrong in 26% of restarts (65% after corners), because the ball reappears already in flight and leaves no touch to see, while the taker is by far the nearest player (median 2 m against 11 m for the nearest opponent). With the rule: 11% wrong (corners 15 of 25 wrong before, 4 after). Worth +0.02 (`label_free`) and +0.014 (`published_baselines`); insensitive to its parameters.
- 2026-11-14 · task 03 · A tracked ball moving at 4 m/s or more inside the pitch counts as evidence of play (+2 nats). On our tracking the ball reappears after a gap already moving (median 11.9 m/s in the first half second). Worth +0.004 to +0.008 on the external matches, consistent across profiles and label readings but inside the noise on its own.
- 2026-11-14 · tasks 03-05 · Considered and not adopted: a prior on which team restarts after a stoppage ("the other team than before"). Measured on the three external matches, the team in possession after a stoppage is the other team in 47% of 298 stoppages (43-49% per match), so at the level of possessions there is nothing to exploit.
- 2026-11-14 · task 07 · If the supervised track is combined with the label-free one, it is combined as evidence before the decoder (`ball_state.blend`, `team.blend`: weighted sum of log-odds), never by voting on two label files, which would create short possessions wherever they disagree. The choice between fusion and a single track is fixed in advance in docs/FUSION.md: fusion only if it passes the acceptance rule against the better single track under both degradation profiles; otherwise the better single track; if neither wins by the rule, the label-free track. The supervised model must be scored leave-one-match-out on the Metrica games.
